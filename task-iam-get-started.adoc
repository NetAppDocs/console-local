= Get started with fleet management in NetApp Console (local)
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
When you install NetApp Console (local), you're prompted to create a new organization. The organization includes one member (an Organization admin) and one default fleet. To set up fleet management to meet your business needs, you'll need to customize your organization's hierarchy, add additional members, add or discover resources, and associate those resources across your hierarchy.

You need the *Org admin* or *Super admin* permissions to set up fleet management for your organization. You configure fleets and grant access with the Identity and access features of Console (local)
Follow these steps to create fleets within your organization and grant access to them. The order may vary based on your organization's needs.

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-1.png[One] Edit the default fleet or add to your organization's hierarchy

[role="quick-margin-para"]
Use the default fleet or create additional fleets and folders matching your business hierarchy.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
link:task-iam-manage-folders-fleets.html[Learn how to organize your resources with folders and fleets].

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-2.png[Two] Associate members with your organization

[role="quick-margin-para"]
When you add a user to  NetApp Console (local), you grant them access to specific fleet(s) by assiging them a role within a fleet or fleets.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
link:task-iam-manage-members-permissions.html[Learn how to manage members and their permissions].

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-3.png[Three] Add or discover resources

[role="quick-margin-para"]
Add or discover resources (systems) to the Console. Organization members manage systems from within a fleet.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
Learn how to create or discover resources:

[role="quick-margin-list"]
* https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/storage-management-fsx-ontap/index.html[Amazon FSx for NetApp ONTAP^]
* https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/storage-management-azure-netapp-files/index.html[Azure NetApp Files^]
* https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/storage-management-cloud-volumes-ontap/index.html[Cloud Volumes ONTAP^]
* https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/storage-management-e-series/index.html[E-Series systems^]
* https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/storage-management-ontap-onprem/index.html[On-premises ONTAP clusters^]
* https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/storage-management-storagegrid/index.html[StorageGRID^]

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-4.png[Four] Associate resources with additional fleets

[role="quick-margin-para"]
Adding or discovering a system in the Console automatically associates the resource with the currently selected fleet. To make that resource available to another fleet in your organization, associate it with the respective fleet. 

[role="quick-margin-list"]
* link:task-iam-manage-resources.html[Learn how to manage your organization's resource hierarchy].
