Alerts in NetApp Console (local) help you find and resolve issues in your on-premises ONTAP clusters. The alerting feature is part of fleet monitoring. You can view alerts for fleets you have permission to access or see all alerts across your organization.

NOTE: This topic is a stub. Expand it based on the alerting functional specification.

== What you can do with alerts

* View alerts at the organization level or for a specific fleet
* Investigate the details of an active alert, including impact area and severity
* Monitor systems health by cluster
* Search, filter, and export alerts
* Enable or disable alert rules
* Receive notifications when alerts occur

== Alert sources

On-premises ONTAP clusters raise alerts through the Harvest integration with NetApp Console (local).
