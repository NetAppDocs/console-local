= Administer NetApp Console (local)

:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
After you deploy NetApp Console (local), use the administration and monitoring tools to review activity, maintain the VM, and manage member access.

== Audit activity

Review user and API activity, track the status of Console operations, download audit logs, and export logs to external tools when you need longer retention or additional analysis.

* link:task-audit-console-activity.html[Audit NetApp Console (local) activity]
* link:task-console-configure-notifications-delivery.html[Set up notifications delivery channels in NetApp Console (local)]

== Licenses and subscriptions

Use the licensing and subscriptions information to understand service entitlements and subscription status for your Console environment.

link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/index.html[Learn about licenses and subscriptions^]

== Maintain and troubleshoot the Console (local) VM

Maintain the VM that hosts Console (local), review network and system settings, access diagnostic tools, and troubleshoot issues when the service or agent does not behave as expected.

link:task-console-local-vm-config.html[Learn about how to maintain your VCenter or ESXi host for Console (local).]

Review member access across your organization, adjust access at the fleet level, and manage security settings such as MFA recovery and service account credentials.

* link:task-iam-manage-roles.html[Manage member access in NetApp Console]
* link:task-iam-users-fleets-assign.html[View or change fleet access assignments in NetApp Console (local)]
* link:task-manage-member-security.html[Manage member security in NetApp Console (local)]
