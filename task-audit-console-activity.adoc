// Jira: SPSPM-3319

Document administrative activity during compliance reviews to verify access changes and preserve a record of actions across NetApp Console local deployment.

.Required roles
Super admin, Organization admin, Folder and fleet admin, Operations support analyst, Super viewer, Organization viewer, or Folder and fleet viewer. link:reference-iam-predefined-roles.html[Learn about access roles].


You can view audit activity in the Console local deployment and download a CSV of the audit logs.




== Audit user activity from the Audit page

Monitor administrative activity to trace changes across your NetApp Console local deployment when resolving incidents or verifying operational accountability.

The Audit page shows the actions that users completed within your organization. For example, you can verify who added a member to an organization, confirm that a user deleted a fleet, or view other storage management actions that users perform in your organization.

The audit logs show activity for the following services:

* Tenancy: actions related to managing your organization, such as adding users, creating fleets, and associating resources.
* Storage-management: actions related to managing your storage resources.
* Federation: actions related to managing federations, such as creating federations and adding or removing federated organizations.


.Steps

. Select *Administration > Audit*.

. Use the filters above the table to narrow the audit activity by time range, service, action, resource, user, or status.

The audit entries show the user who performed the action when the user's identity can be resolved. If the user's identity cannot be resolved, the entry shows the user's `auth0Id` instead of their name.
+
For example, you can use the *Service* filter to show actions related to a specific service, or you can use the *User* filter to show actions related to a specific user account.

=== Filter audit logs for identity and access management actions

Review access-management changes to confirm who modified organizational resources and support accurate security investigations.

.Steps

. Select *Administration > Audit*.

. Use the filters to narrow the results. Select *Service* and then select *Tenancy*.

. Use any of the other filters to further refine the results.
+
For example, use the *User* filter to show IAM actions performed by a specific user account.

=== Download audit logs from the Audit page
Preserve an independent record of organizational actions to support compliance reviews and future incident investigations. The downloaded CSV file includes all audit log columns, even if you apply filters or change the displayed columns.

.Steps
. In the *Audit* page, select the download icon in the upper right corner of the table.
