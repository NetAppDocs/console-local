= Storage monitoring in NetApp Console (local)

:nofooter:

NetApp Console (local) provides fleet-wide health dashboards, deep-dive volume analysis, configurable alerts, and guided or automated remediation so you can identify and resolve storage issues before they affect workloads.

== Fleet-wide health and performance

Monitor the health and performance of all volumes across your fleets from a single dashboard. Quickly identify clusters or volumes that need attention.
