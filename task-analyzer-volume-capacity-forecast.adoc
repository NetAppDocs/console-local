= Forecast volume capacity and plan for growth in NetApp Console Local
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Use the Workload Analyzer to review how a volume's capacity has consumed over time and project when it will reach warning or critical thresholds. You can also check whether snapshot consumption is trending toward overflowing its reserve.

.Steps

. In the NetApp Console Local, navigate to *Workload Analyzer*.
+
Alternatively, from the storage inventory, select a volume and click *Analyze*.

. In the *Volume* field, search for and select the volume you want to analyze.

. Set the *Time Range* to *Last 7 days* to capture enough growth history for a meaningful forecast, then click *Analyze*.

. Scroll to the *Capacity Analysis* section.

. Enable *Show Forecast*.
+
The chart extends beyond the current time with a dashed projection line. An insight message appears when the forecast indicates the volume will reach the 85% warning threshold or 95% critical threshold, along with an estimated number of days until the threshold is reached.

. Hover over a point on the capacity chart to see the current breakdown:
+
* Physical capacity consumed
* Available space remaining
* Storage efficiency ratio, including savings from deduplication, compression, and compaction

. To review snapshot space separately, open the *View* dropdown and select *Snapshot View*.

. In the snapshot view, enable *Show Forecast* to project when snapshot consumption will exceed the configured snapshot reserve.
+
If snapshots are projected to overflow the reserve, they will begin consuming space from the volume's data area, which accelerates capacity consumption.

.After you finish

If the volume is projected to fill within your operational planning window, consider one of the following actions:

* Increase the volume size
* Adjust the tiering policy to move cold data to a capacity tier
* Increase the snapshot reserve or reduce the snapshot retention schedule
* Move the volume to an aggregate with more available space

See link:task-remediation-guided.html[Remediate with guided steps] for assisted options.
