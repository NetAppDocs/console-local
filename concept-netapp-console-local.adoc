= Learn about NetApp Console local deployment
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
// Jira: SPSPM-3319

NetApp Console local deployment is a self-hosted deployment option that lets you run the NetApp Console local deployment control plane in your on-premises environment.

NetApp Console local deployment delivers the unified storage management and automation capabilities such as provisioning, policy enforcement, drift detection, and remediation in an isolated deployment that keeps your data, metadata, and management traffic entirely within your own infrastructure. You get operational leverage without sacrificing the data sovereignty your organization requires.


== Fleet-wide monitoring and observability

NetApp Console local deployment gives you a single place to watch the health and performance of every cluster you manage. Fleet-wide dashboards surface the clusters and volumes that need attention, configurable alerts notify the right people when conditions change, and custom dashboards let each administrator build the monitoring view that matches their responsibilities. When a volume needs a closer look, the Workload Analyzer correlates latency, throughput, resource utilization, and configuration changes over time so you can pinpoint root causes and act before issues affect workloads.

link:concept-monitor-storage.html[Learn about monitoring storage health in NetApp Console local deployment]

== Alert delivery integrations

NetApp Console local deployment helps you route alert notifications to the right teams through configurable delivery channels. Organization admins can configure email and webhook destinations, and storage admins can apply those channels in alert rules so response paths match your operational model. This helps enterprise teams reduce noise and respond faster to capacity, performance, and protection events.

link:task-console-configure-notifications-delivery.html[Set up notification delivery channels in NetApp Console local deployment]
link:task-alert-rules-manage.html[Configure notification rules for alerts in NetApp Console local deployment]


== Policy-driven storage provisioning

NetApp Console local deployment enforces consistent storage behavior through storage classes—templates that bundle performance, capacity, and tiering policies into reusable definitions. Administrators define storage standards once. Provisioning activity across your environment, whether manual or automated, draws from those approved classes. This eliminates configuration drift, reduces the time junior administrators spend on provisioning decisions, and ensures that every workload meets your organization's standards from day one.

link:concept-manage-storage.html[Learn about managing storage with NetApp Console local deployment]

== Continuous compliance monitoring and automated remediation

After provisioning, NetApp Console local deployment monitors every workload against the storage class it was provisioned with. When a workload drifts—because of a direct cluster configuration change or degrading performance—NetApp Console local deployment flags the violation immediately. Administrators can follow guided remediation steps or configure automated remediation to resolve common drift conditions without manual intervention. This continuous enforcement reduces audit risk and keeps your environment compliant between scheduled reviews.

== Local deployment operations and connectivity

NetApp Console local deployment runs in your own infrastructure, so your team controls deployment, connectivity, and day-2 operations. You deploy the Console agent, maintain the Console virtual machine, and manage the network paths required for monitoring and management traffic. This gives enterprise administrators full control of operational boundaries while keeping management data inside the environment.

== Data protection

Beyond provisioning and monitoring, NetApp Console local deployment gives you access to NetApp Backup and Recovery so you can protect your data from the same interface. You can back up and restore your data to support your protection and recovery requirements. Managing data protection alongside your storage keeps governance consistent and reduces the number of tools your teams need to operate.

link:concept-data-services.html[Learn about data services in NetApp Console local deployment]

== AI-assisted management

NetApp Console local deployment can connect to a large language model (LLM) of your choice to provide AI-assisted storage management. You can describe a workload in plain language to get a provisioning plan, ask the Console to investigate an active alert, or get contextual answers about your storage environment. Every AI action stays within the guardrails of your storage classes and the role-based access controls assigned to your account, and sensitive operations require your confirmation. Requests are sent only to the LLM endpoint your administrators configure for the Console local deployment.

link:concept-llm-integration.html[Learn about LLM integration in NetApp Console local deployment]

== Role-based access control and identity integration

NetApp Console local deployment provides granular role-based access control (RBAC) that scopes what users can see and do to the fleets and resources they are responsible for. You can integrate with Active Directory so users sign in with their existing corporate credentials, and local users can add multi-factor authentication (MFA) for another layer of verification. Access governance stays aligned with your organization's broader identity and access management practices.

link:concept-identity-and-access-management.html[Learn about identity and access management in NetApp Console local deployment]
link:concept-secure-access.html[Learn about secure access to NetApp Console local deployment]

== Enterprise resource organization and delegated administration

NetApp Console local deployment scales administration by organizing resources into folders and fleets. You can map fleets to environments, business units, or regions, then delegate administration at organization, folder, or fleet scope to keep ownership clear. This structure helps large storage teams distribute work without losing policy consistency or governance.

link:concept-folders-fleets-overview.html[Learn about folders and fleets in NetApp Console local deployment]
link:concept-storage-fleets.html[Learn about storage fleets in NetApp Console local deployment]

== API-driven operations and service accounts

NetApp Console local deployment supports API-based integrations so your organization can automate repeatable storage operations. Service accounts let applications authenticate and operate with assigned roles, and those actions remain governed by the same RBAC and audit controls used for interactive users. This supports enterprise automation while keeping access scoped and accountable.

link:task-iam-manage-members-permissions.html[Add members to your NetApp Console local deployment organization]

== Audit trail and log export

Every administrative action in NetApp Console local deployment generates an audit record, giving your security and compliance teams the visibility they need to investigate incidents, demonstrate control effectiveness, and satisfy audit requirements. You can export the audit logs to your own syslog server through a webhook so they reach your centralized monitoring or SIEM tools for longer retention and analysis. Because NetApp Console local deployment runs in your own environment, log data never leaves your infrastructure.

link:task-audit-console-activity.html[Learn about auditing activity in NetApp Console local deployment]
