= Learn about LLM integration in NetApp Console (local deployment)
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
// Jira: SPSPM-3325, SPSPM-3304

The NetApp Console (local deployment) can connect to a large language model (LLM) of your choice to provide AI-assisted storage management. Once connected, you can use natural language to provision storage, investigate alerts, and get answers to storage management questions—without leaving the Console (local deployment).

AI-assisted management allows you to describe what you need in plain language and have the Console (local deployment) act on it, guided by the storage policies your organization has already defined.

Because the NetApp Console (local deployment) runs in your own environment, your data and queries stay within your network at all times. Console (local deployment) does not send your data to a third-party service without your explicit configuration.

== What you can do with AI-assisted management

When LLM integration is enabled, the Console (local deployment) provides three AI-assisted capabilities:

* *Storage provisioning* Describe a workload's requirements in plain language, and the Console (local deployment) recommends and executes a provisioning plan based on your configured storage classes.
* *Alert response*  Ask the Console (local deployment) to investigate an active alert, and it will analyze the issue and suggest a fix or perform an action, depending on the permissions you've granted.
* *Search and guidance* Ask questions about your storage environment or ONTAP administration, and the Console (local deployment) returns contextual answers drawn from documentation and your fleet's current state.

All AI actions respect the role-based access controls assigned to your user account. Sensitive operations require your confirmation before the Console (local deployment) proceeds.

== Prerequisites

Before connecting an LLM, ensure the following are in place:

* Deploy and run the NetApp Console (local deployment)
* Ensure the Console (local deployment) has access to the clusters you want to manage with AI assistance
* Subscribe to a supported LLM provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or AWS Bedrock) and obtain a valid API key
* Configure your network to allow outbound connections from the Console (local deployment) to your LLM provider's endpoint
* Have the Organization admin role in the NetApp Console (local deployment)
* Configure and assign storage classes in the Console (local deployment) so the AI can guide provisioning decisions
