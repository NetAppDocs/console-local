= Learn about identity and access management
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Learn about the NetApp Console Local identity and access management (IAM) capabilities, including user roles, the folder and fleet resource hierarchy, federation, single sign-on, and multi-factor authentication.

The NetApp Console Local uses IAM to organize your storage resources and control access according to your business structure. You organize resources in a hierarchy: the organization is at the top, then folders (which can contain other folders or fleets), and then fleets, which contain storage systems.

Assign access roles at the organization, folder, or fleet level so users have the right access to resources.

NOTE: You must have the Organization admin or Folder or fleet admin role to manage IAM in the NetApp Console Local.

=== Resource hierarchy components

* *Organization*: The top level of the IAM hierarchy, representing your company. Your organization consists of folders, fleets, members, roles, and resources.
* *Folders*: Group related fleets within your organization. Create folders for regions, business units, or teams. You can nest folders within other folders. Assigning a role at the folder level gives the member access to all fleets in that folder. Folders cannot have storage systems directly associated with them.
* *Fleets*: Group the storage systems that you manage together. Assign resources to fleets and grant users access at the fleet level. Resources can belong to multiple fleets.
