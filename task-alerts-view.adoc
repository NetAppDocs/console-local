= View alerts in NetApp Console Local
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
View active alerts in the NetApp Console Local from the alerts overview page. By default, the page shows an organization-wide view; you can switch to a fleet-wide view to focus on alerts for a specific fleet.

== Before you begin

You must have permission to view alerts for the organization or fleet.

.Steps

. From the left navigation, select *Alerts*.
. From the scope selector, choose one of the following:
+
* *Organization* (default) to see alerts across all fleets you have access to
* A specific fleet to see alerts only for that fleet

. Review the alert summary, including:
+
* Alert distribution by severity
* Alert distribution by impact area
* Critical alerts grouped by impact area
* Alert count distribution over the past 24 hours

. Select the *Alerts* tab to see the full list of active alerts.
