= Identify QoS policy throttling on a volume in NetApp Console Local
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
When a volume's performance is constrained, the cause may be a QoS policy limiting its IOPS or throughput. Use the Workload Analyzer to compare actual workload demand against the QoS policy limits attached to the volume.

.Before you begin

A QoS policy must be attached to the volume. If no policy is attached, QoS limit lines do not appear in the analyzer.

.Steps

. In the NetApp Console Local, navigate to *Workload Analyzer*.
+
Alternatively, from the storage inventory, select a volume and click *Analyze*.

. In the *Volume* field, search for and select the volume you want to investigate.

. Set the *Time Range* to cover the period of the reported performance issue, then click *Analyze*.

. In the *Latency Analysis* section, use the metric picker to enable *QoS Limit Max*.
+
The QoS maximum latency ceiling appears as a horizontal reference line on the chart. If the latency line is approaching or touching this line, the workload is being throttled.

. In the *Throughput Analysis* section, use the component picker to enable *QoS IOPS Limit* and *QoS MB/s Limit*.
+
The policy limits appear as horizontal reference lines on the IOPS and MB/s charts. Compare actual throughput against these lines to see how much headroom remains.

. Hover over a point on the throughput chart where IOPS is highest to see the read, write, and other breakdown and compare the total against the QoS IOPS limit line.

. Enable *Show Forecast* in the throughput section to project whether demand is trending toward the QoS limit.

.Results

If the workload is consistently at or near the QoS limit, the volume is being throttled. Options to resolve this include:

* Increasing the QoS policy maximum if the cluster has available headroom
* Moving the volume to a cluster with more capacity
* Associating the volume with a storage class that has a higher-performance policy

See link:task-remediation-guided.html[Remediate with guided steps] for assisted options.
